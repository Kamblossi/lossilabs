What we do

At Lossi Labs, we advance bold research at the intersection of aging biology, chemistry, and AI, drawing on deep expertise in drug discovery and development to redefine what’s possible in human health and aging.

We are building a pipeline of innovative small molecule therapies by decoding complex biology. Inspired by the groundbreaking work of our co-founder, Prof. Jim Collins at MIT, our team’s research has been repeatedly highlighted in Nature, Nature Aging, Nature Protocols, and Cell.

Today, we focus on combating age-related diseases, with the long-term goal of transforming the fundamental biology of aging.
