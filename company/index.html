What we do

At Lossi Labs, we combine deep systems engineering, database optimization, and applied AI to build custom software across engineering, healthcare, enterprise operations, and modern business workflows.

Our current build pipeline spans specialized domain-specific platforms, real-time desktop AI assistants, modular ERP systems, and high-throughput backend infrastructure. Across healthcare, engineering, finance, and enterprise operations, we embed autonomous agents and intelligent automation directly into core backend processes—turning complex, multi-step workflows into self-executing pipelines.

Under the hood, our work centers on solving real systems bottlenecks—optimizing database query execution, building fault-tolerant multi-platform sync across web, mobile, and desktop, and architecting lean cloud deployments capable of handling sustained production traffic without stalling.
